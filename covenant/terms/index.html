6. Safety

Never send money to someone you met on Covenant. Requests for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency or bank details are the single most common pattern in romance fraud, including from people who seem entirely sincere and who have spoken to you for months. There is no situation in which sending money to someone you have not met is safe. We will warn you when a conversation mentions it, but the warning is not a substitute for the rule.

When you first meet in person, meet somewhere public, tell someone where you are going, and arrange your own transport. Covenant provides trusted contacts and check-ins for exactly this, and using them costs nothing.

You can report or block any member at any time. Reports are confidential and the member is never told who reported them. Blocking closes every route between you: messages, calls, status and discovery.

Verification is not a background check. We do not screen members for criminal history, and a badge is not a statement that someone is safe, honest or unmarried. Use your own judgement.
