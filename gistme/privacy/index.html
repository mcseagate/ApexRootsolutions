Privacy Policy

DRAFT — effective on publication · Drafted 1 October 2026

GistMe is a voice-calling app for one-to-one calls, designed to work on weak and expensive connections, published by Apex Root Solutions LLC. This policy describes what the app and its servers actually do with your information.

The short version. We do not record or store your calls. Call audio travels directly between the two phones, or through a relay that only forwards encrypted data. We never read your address book, location or advertising ID. We keep an account (username, display name), your contacts and a short call log so the app works. There is no advertising and no third-party analytics.

3. What we do not collect

  • Call audio — we do not record it and it never reaches our application servers.
  • Your address book or phone number, your location, advertising identifiers, or the contents of any call.
  • Third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting data. The app contains no such SDKs.

4. How calls work, and who can see what

Call setup messages (who is calling, connection details) go to our servers over an encrypted connection. Audio is encrypted with DTLS-SRTP and sent directly to the other phone when possible; otherwise it passes through a relay server that forwards encrypted data it cannot decrypt. When a call is direct, the other person's phone can learn your IP address; turn on Always relay my calls in Settings to prevent that.

The encryption protects call audio between the two phones. Because the connection details that authenticate the two phones pass through our servers, GistMe does not claim end-to-end encryption with verified identities. You can compare the security code shown during the call with the other person to confirm that nobody is in the middle.

5. Who we share data with

  • Apple (APNs) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) deliver the wake-up notification for incoming calls. They receive your device's push token and an opaque notification, not call content.
  • Hosting and relay providers that run our servers process data on our behalf under contract.
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect safety. We cannot hand over call audio because we do not have it.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Security

Connections use TLS; credentials are held in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore; passwords are hashed with Argon2id; we rate-limit sign-in, lookups, invites and call attempts. No system is perfectly secure.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in Settings → Account. This deletes your profile, contacts, invites and sessions; other people's call logs show "Deleted user".
  • Delete call history entries, sign out of devices, change your password, block and report people.
  • Ask for access, correction or deletion of data we hold by writing to support@apsoluts.com. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights, which we honour.

Lost password: because we do not collect an email address or phone number, a lost password and lost recovery code cannot be recovered. Keep your recovery code safe.

8. Children

GistMe is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. Do not use GistMe if you are under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

9. Data costs and connectivity

Calls use your Wi-Fi or mobile data. GistMe is designed to use little data but cannot make calls without an internet connection. The app shows an estimate of each call's data use and explains how it is measured.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
