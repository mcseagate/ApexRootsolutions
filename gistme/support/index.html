Help with GistMe. Write to support@apsoluts.com.

Calls don't ring when the app is closed

On Android, a force-stopped app cannot receive calls; open GistMe once to re-enable it, allow notifications, and set Battery to "Unrestricted" (some phone makers add extra "auto-start" switches). On iPhone, allow notifications and keep the app installed; if you use Focus modes, allow calls from your contacts.

Lost password or recovery code

Use Recover account with your username and recovery code. We do not hold an email or phone number for you, so if both are lost the account cannot be recovered; create a new one.

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → Delete account. This is permanent.

Reporting abuse

Open the person's contact page and choose Report, or write to us. Reports contain call metadata only; there is no audio to attach because calls are not recorded.

How much data does a call use?

It depends on the audio mode and your network. The app shows an estimate after each call and explains what it includes. A low audio bitrate is not the same as low data use: packet headers, security and connection checks add traffic.
