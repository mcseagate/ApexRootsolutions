GistMe

Delete your account

DRAFT — effective on publication · Drafted 2 October 2026

You can delete your GistMe account and its data yourself, in the app, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Settings and scroll to Account.
  2. Choose Delete account and enter your password to confirm.

Deleting is permanent and signs you out on every device.

What is deleted

Immediately: your account (username, display name, password and recovery-code hashes), your devices and push tokens, your contacts, contact requests, invite links and blocks. Calls you took part in remain in the other person's call log, for up to 90 days, as "Deleted user", no longer linked to any account. Reports filed by or about you are kept unlinked for up to 365 days. Encrypted database backups are kept for up to 30 days, so deleted data can persist in a backup for up to 30 days; if we ever restore from a backup, we delete accounts that were deleted after it was taken again as soon as we can. See the Privacy Policy for the full list.

If you cannot sign in

Use Recover account in the app with your username and recovery code, then delete the account. We hold no email address or phone number for you, so we cannot verify that an account is yours without your password or recovery code, and we cannot delete it on request without one of them. Write to support@apsoluts.com if you need to talk it through.
