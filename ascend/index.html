Ascend

An app-marketing platform for developers and agencies.

Ascend analyses public App Store and Google Play listings, drafts recommendations, listing copy, ad concepts and social posts, and — only after a person approves each item — publishes them through the official APIs of the platforms you connect.

Nothing is published, launched, activated, spent or posted without an individual confirmation, and every such action is written to an audit log you can export. Ascend never asks for a platform password: connections use OAuth or keys the platform issues, encrypted at rest and revocable at any time. It does not create or manipulate reviews and will not build tooling to evade store policy.
