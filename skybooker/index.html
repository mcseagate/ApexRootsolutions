SkyBooker

SkyBooker compares flights, stays and car hire across sellers, shows the full price with taxes, fees and baggage up front, and hands you to the seller to book.

SkyBooker is a comparison app, not a travel agent. It searches partner networks for the trip you describe, lays the results side by side — including the things that are usually hidden until checkout, such as a first checked bag fee or a self-transfer connection — and, when you choose one, opens the seller's own booking page. Your booking, your payment and your ticket are with that seller.

There is no account and no tracking. Trips you save are kept on your device.
