Common questions

I lost my notes when I changed device

Your study material is stored on the device and is deliberately kept out of iCloud and Android backups, so that "keep this on my device" means what it says. That also means a device transfer does not carry it across. Export from the Library before you move, or sign in so syncing keeps a copy. If you have neither, we cannot recover it, because we never held it.

The assistant says it cannot answer

It needs to send your question to answer it. If What leaves this device is set to Nothing leaves this device, the assistant is off by design. It will also say so plainly if the network is unavailable — reading, search and your own notes keep working either way.

Can I use a passage or a commentary quotation in something I publish?

Check the resource's entry in the Library first. Each one records its edition, licence, copyright notice and attribution, and some carry obligations that travel with the text. The Terms of Use cover this in section 4.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Library → Account → Delete my account. It takes effect at once. If you can no longer use the app, write to support@apsoluts.com from the account's email address. Deleting your account explains what is removed and what is kept. Anything kept only on your device stays on your device, under your control.

How do I report a post or a member?

Open the post and choose Report, or block the member so you no longer see what they write. We review every report within 24 hours.
