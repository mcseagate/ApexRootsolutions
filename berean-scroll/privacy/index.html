Berean Scroll

Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026 · Last updated 5 September 2026

Berean Scroll is a Bible reading and study app published by Apex Root Solutions LLC. This policy explains what the app keeps on your device, what it sends, and what happens to anything it sends. It describes the app as it actually behaves, not as a category of app generally behaves.

The short version. Reading and study work with no account and no network. Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading plans, memory cards and prayer journal are stored on your device and are deliberately excluded from device backups. There is no advertising, no tracking, and no analytics of any kind. Nothing leaves your device unless a feature you use needs it to — and you can switch that off entirely.

1. Who we are

Apex Root Solutions LLC, a limited liability company organised under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its registered office in Wilmington, New Castle County, Delaware, and its principal place of business in Cedar Hill, Dallas County, Texas ("we", "us") publishes Berean Scroll and is the controller of the personal data described here.

Contact us at support@apsoluts.com for any question about this policy, or to exercise any right described in section 9.

2. What stays on your device

The following are created and stored locally, and are not transmitted to us unless you sign in and enable syncing (section 4):

  • Notes, highlights, bookmarks and tags
  • Reading plans and your progress through them
  • Memory cards and their review schedule
  • Prayer journal entries
  • Exegetical worksheets, study projects and word lists
  • Reading position, history and reader settings
  • Downloaded Scripture editions and study resources

This work is also excluded from the operating system's own backups — iCloud and iTunes backups on iOS, and Android backup and device-to-device transfer. This is deliberate. The app lets you mark material as staying on your device, and that promise cannot hold if a nightly backup copies it elsewhere.

The cost of that choice is yours to manage. If you lose your device and have not exported your work, it is gone. We cannot recover it, because we never had it. Export from the Library, to somewhere you control, or sign in so that syncing keeps a copy.

3. Turning sending off completely

The Library contains a setting, What leaves this device, with a mode called Nothing leaves this device. In that mode the app makes no network requests at all. Reading, notes, highlights, plans, memory cards and the prayer journal are unaffected — they are already local.

What stops working is stated in the app before you choose it: syncing, the AI assistant, downloading new editions, communities, and signing in.

4. What is sent, and when

Each item below happens only when you use that feature.

FeatureWhat is sentWhat we keep
Account Email address and password Your email address and a cryptographic hash of your password. We never store the password itself.
Syncing Notes, highlights, bookmarks and related study material, each anchored to a Scripture reference Stored against your account so your devices agree. Deleted when you delete the item or your account.
AI assistant Your question, the mode you chose, and the passage text and references the app selected to ground the answer Nothing. The question and its sources build one request and are not stored. Only a per-day count of questions asked survives, so that free-tier limits can be enforced.
Communities What you post, comment or share into a community Stored and shown to the members of that community, under its moderators.
Downloading resources A request for the catalogue and for the files you choose Ordinary web server logs, including IP address, kept briefly for security and reliability.

The AI assistant in particular

When you ask the assistant a question, it is sent to our server, which adds the passages the answer must be grounded in and forwards the request to Google's Gemini API. Google processes it under its own terms as our processor. We do not send your notes, your prayer journal, your identity, or anything else you have not asked about.

Answers are labelled as AI-generated throughout the app. The assistant is a research aid and is not Scripture, a pastor, or a doctrinal authority; see the Terms of Use.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no sale or sharing of personal data with advertisers.
  • No analytics or product-telemetry service. The app contains no analytics provider — not Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, Sentry, or any other.
  • No third-party trackers, SDKs or advertising identifiers.
  • No profiling, and no automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.
  • No reading of your notes or prayer journal by us. Where syncing stores them, they are stored to be given back to you.

6. Notifications

Reading reminders are scheduled on your device. You control how much a reminder reveals on the lock screen: the reading itself, only that something is due, or nothing at all. A reminder that names the passage has shown it to everyone who can see your phone, which is why the choice exists.

7. Children

Berean Scroll is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Where a church, school or other institution enrols minors in a course or community, that institution is responsible for obtaining the consent its own law requires, and acts as controller for the student records it creates. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to support@apsoluts.com and we will delete it.

8. Security and retention

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are hashed. Authentication tokens are held in the platform's secure storage — the iOS Keychain and the Android Keystore-backed store.

We keep account data for as long as the account exists. Synced study material is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Community posts remain visible to their community until deleted by you or a moderator. Server logs are kept briefly. AI questions are not retained at all; the per-day counter is kept only as long as it is needed to enforce a daily limit.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. Turning sending off stops the app transmitting; it does not encrypt your device or protect anything if your unlocked phone is in someone else's hand.

9. Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data. Much of this is in the app: export your work from the Library, and delete any note, highlight or entry directly. To delete your account and everything synced with it, write to support@apsoluts.com.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to a supervisory authority. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (your account, syncing, communities), legitimate interests (security, reliability, preventing abuse), and consent where we ask for it. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are used in California law.

10. International transfers

We operate from Texas, in the United States, and data we process is processed in the United States. Google's Gemini API may process AI requests in other countries. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA or the UK, we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how we handle your data, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
