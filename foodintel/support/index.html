Support

Help with the FoodIntel app — subscriptions, your account, and your data.

Contact us

Email support@apsoluts.com and we will respond within a few business days. Including your device type (iPhone/Android) and a short description of the problem helps us help you faster.

Subscriptions & billing

  • Manage or cancel — iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues to the end of the paid period.
  • Restore purchases — open the app's paywall or profile screen and choose Restore. Purchases are tied to the Apple/Google account used to buy them.
  • Refunds — processed by Apple or Google under their respective policies; contact them directly or reach out to us for guidance.

Free tier

The free tier includes 3 new product lookups per day. Products you have already looked up stay available in your diary, history, and favorites without limit. The counter resets every day at local midnight.

Account & data

  • Delete your account — Profile → Account → Delete account. This removes your server-side account data.
  • Your diary and profiles — stored only on your device and deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Sign-in issues — try signing out and back in; if an email reset is needed, contact us.

Product information

Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a community-maintained open database. If a product is missing or incorrect, it can be improved directly at openfoodfacts.org, and the change will flow into the app.
