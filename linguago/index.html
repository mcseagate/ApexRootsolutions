LinguaGo

LinguaGo translates what you type, say, point the camera at, or hand it as a document — and tells you, every single time, which engine produced the answer and how much to trust it.

Most translation apps give you a sentence and no way to judge it. LinguaGo labels every result with the engine behind it, flags low confidence instead of presenting a guess as a certainty, and says plainly when a language pair has no offline model rather than letting you discover it with no signal.

Download a language and it works with no connection at all. Hold the phone between two people and each speaks their own language. Point it at a menu and the translation is drawn over the words in the sign's own colours. Plan a trip and it paces the phrases worth knowing to your departure date.
