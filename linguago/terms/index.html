4. What a translation is, and is not

This section matters more than the rest.

LinguaGo produces machine translations, either from a model running on your device or from an AI model reached through our server. Machine translation is useful and it is not reliable. It can be fluent and wrong at the same time, which is the failure mode worth taking seriously: output that reads confidently while saying something the original did not.

Accordingly:

  • LinguaGo is provided for general and travel use. It is not a certified, sworn or legal translation, and must not be submitted anywhere one is required.
  • Do not rely on it for medical, legal, financial, safety-critical or immigration decisions, or for anything where a mistranslation could cause harm. Use a qualified human translator.
  • Quality varies enormously by language pair. The app labels every result with the engine that produced it and flags low confidence, precisely so you can judge what you are looking at rather than being asked to trust a single number.
  • Some languages the app will attempt are poorly served by any current model, and a few defeat them entirely.

You are responsible for deciding whether a given translation is fit for what you are about to do with it.
