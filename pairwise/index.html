PairWise

PairWise is a structured way for two people to work through conflict, rebuild trust, and decide together what happens next. It does not take sides, it does not pretend to be a therapist, and it keeps what each person writes privately to that person.

Each partner has a private space the other cannot open — check-ins, a journal, reflections, safety answers. Between them is a shared space that only receives what one of them deliberately puts there, with a record of who shared what and when. The guided conversation is turn-taking: one speaks, the other says back what they heard, the first confirms or corrects, and only then do they swap.

If something someone writes suggests they may not be safe, PairWise stops being couples work: it shows support lines, switches off joint sessions on that device, and does not tell the other partner.
