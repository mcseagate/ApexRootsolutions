QuoteShield

QuoteShield is a private record-keeper for a home improvement project: the quotes you receive, what each one does and does not cover, the agreement you sign, what you have paid, what changed, and what is still open. It lives entirely on your phone.

QuoteShield does not rate contractors, does not tell you which quote to take, does not check licences or insurance, and does not send anything anywhere. It arranges what you typed and photographed so that you can see gaps, ask better questions, and keep a clear timeline if something goes wrong. Where a number is unknown it says so rather than guessing zero.
