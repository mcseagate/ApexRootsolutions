Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026. This page describes the data Fit actually holds; the formal privacy notice is in legal review.

What Fit collects

Without an account: nothing that identifies you. Comparisons need a destination country, and a postal code only if you choose to enter one for shipping estimates — never a precise location. With an account: your email address, your saved items and alerts, your notification preferences, and a device token if you enable push in a mobile app.

Measurement

Fit counts events such as “a search returned results” as daily totals with no identifier, no IP address and no search text attached, so they cannot be traced back to a person. There is no third-party tracker on any page, and no advertising network.

How long it is kept

Observed prices are kept for the period each retailer's terms permit. Account data is kept until you delete your account; deletion is scheduled with a 30-day grace period and then removes your account, saved items, alerts and devices. Support messages are kept while the case is open plus 90 days.

Your choices

Export everything Fit holds about you as JSON, turn either notification channel off, unsubscribe in one click from any notification email, or delete your account — all from the account page, without contacting anyone.

Who else sees it

Retailers receive nothing about you from Fit beyond the click that sends you to their site. Fit does not sell personal data.

The complete privacy notice is still in legal review. Until it is published here, the statements above describe what the service actually does.
