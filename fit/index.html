Fit

Compare the total you would actually pay, for the exact size and colour you want

Fit is a price-comparison service for branded sportswear and general outfit. It shows what the same variant costs across participating retailers, so the cheapest option is the cheapest one that actually fits.

Where Fit is today. Fit is built and tested but has not launched, and it has no audience yet. The comparisons in Fit's test builds use fictional demonstration data. Real retailer data will be loaded only from the approved feeds and APIs of affiliate networks, under each advertiser's own terms.

What Fit does

Pick an exact variant

Choose size system, size, colour and fit. Fit compares that variant, not a loosely similar product.

See the total, not just the price

Item price, shipping and, where known, tax are added up for your destination. Where a part is unknown, Fit says so instead of guessing a total.

Every price carries its age

Each price shows when it was last observed. A price or stock state is only called current when the retailer's feed or API says so. If a price has changed by the time you click through, Fit shows the revised price before you continue.

You buy from the retailer

Fit does not sell products, take payment or hold inventory. You are handed off to the retailer, whose checkout, delivery, returns and refunds apply.

Web, iOS and Android

Fit is available as a web app and as native iOS and Android apps. The launch market is the United States, in US dollars, in branded sportswear. Other markets would be added only where the corresponding programmes are approved.

How Fit gets its data

Only approved feeds and APIs

Product and price data comes only through affiliate networks' approved feeds or APIs. Fit does not crawl or scrape retailer sites.

Each advertiser's terms apply

Permitted uses, update cadence, retention period, and image and price-history rights are stored per source, and ingestion respects them. If an advertiser withdraws access, Fit pauses that source and handles cached data as the agreement requires.

Images only where permitted

Images are copied and shown only for advertisers whose terms grant image rights.

How Fit is paid

Outbound links carry the affiliate network's tracking link, and an affiliate disclosure is shown next to every outbound shopping link on web, iOS and Android. Commission never affects which offers are shown or how they are ranked.
