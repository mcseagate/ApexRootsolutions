MediVerify AI

MediVerify AI helps you understand symptoms, diagnoses, lab reports, medications and care options in calm, plain language — a second opinion to take into a conversation with your clinician, not a replacement for one.

The app does not diagnose and does not treat. It explains what a result or a term usually means, what questions are worth asking, and where the reputable public sources say more. Before any analysis, it screens what you entered for emergency warning signs and, if it finds one, stops and points you to emergency services first.

Your health information is stored on your device, encrypted, behind your device's biometric or passcode lock, and every consent you give is recorded where you can see it.
