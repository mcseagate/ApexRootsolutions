Screenshot Inbox

Screenshot Inbox turns the screenshots piling up in your photo library into reminders, events, saved links, contacts and searchable records — then helps you safely delete the originals.

Screenshots are a capture reflex: a concert date, a recipe, a tracking number, a return deadline. But a screenshot is an image, so it is not searchable, it cannot remind you of anything, and it does not know it contains a date. The information is lost in practice, and the library fills up with things nobody dares delete.

The app reads each screenshot on your device, works out what it is, and proposes the obvious next step. You confirm; it acts. Then it offers to clear away the original, once what mattered is safely out.
