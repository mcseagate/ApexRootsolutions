Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app works fully with no account, entering figures yourself. An account is not required even to link a bank or card — that connection is tied to your installation of the app.

I turned on bank linking. What exactly did that send?

Only what is needed to open your bank's own sign-in screen and, once you approve it, a read-only connection to the accounts you chose. The Privacy Policy, section 3, sets out exactly what is sent and what is kept at each step.

How do I disconnect a linked bank?

Open Accounts & Cash, select the connected institution, and disconnect it. This revokes our access immediately and removes the connection from our server.

Can the app move money, pay a bill, or make a trade?

No. LifeOS Finance has no ability to initiate a payment, transfer or trade, under any setting. It is a planning and organisation tool.

Is the Copilot's answer financial advice?

No. It runs the same calculations available elsewhere in the app on your own figures, and states its method and assumptions alongside the answer. See the Terms of Use, sections 4 and 6.

How do I delete everything?

Data you entered by hand lives on your device — delete the app to remove it. If you linked a bank or card, disconnect it first (above) so the access grant on our server is deleted too; deleting the app alone does not reach our server.
