LifeOS Finance

A personal finance planning app.

Budgeting, net worth, goals, debt, insurance and planning tools that work from figures you enter yourself. Linking a bank or card is optional, and off until you turn it on. The app never moves money — it cannot make a payment, a transfer or a trade on your behalf.

No account is required to use the app. Financial figures you enter or connect are kept on your device. If you link a bank or card, that connection is read-only and handled through Plaid, described in the Privacy Policy.
